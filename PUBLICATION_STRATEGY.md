# Publication Strategy

## Purpose

Define how KreyolBench should move from scaffold to publishable research.

## Status

Current status: SUBMITTED_TO_REVIEW

Owner: Codex

Requires expert validation: Yes

## Target Venues

Primary targets:

- ACL
- EMNLP
- NAACL
- LREC-COLING
- AfricaNLP
- NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks

Secondary targets:

- JOSS for software infrastructure
- Data & Policy for governance and public-interest benchmark design
- ICTD or ACM COMPASS for applied public-interest systems

## Publication Types

- Dataset/benchmark paper
- Resource paper
- Evaluation paper
- Software paper
- Workshop paper
- Shared-task or leaderboard report

## Contribution Claims

Potential claims must be evidence-backed:

- Kreyol-first benchmark design
- provenance-aware source registry
- public-interest domain coverage
- orthographic variation and code-switch awareness
- reproducible evaluation harness
- baseline matrix and error analysis

Do not claim:

- complete Haitian Creole coverage
- representative national dialect coverage
- production-ready model performance
- unrestricted dataset openness
- superiority over other benchmarks without direct comparison

## Artifact Traceability

Every table, figure, metric, and experiment intended for publication should be mapped in `reports/PAPER_OUTLINE.md`.
