## Purpose

This packet consolidates public metadata findings for all 21 non-synthetic source candidates registered on the review date. The synthetic `sample` fixture is a separate software-control record. The packet supports human selection of later review work; it is not legal advice and does not approve source use.

## Methodological Boundary

- Official pages, terms, repositories, archival catalogs, dataset cards, and primary papers were inspected as metadata.
- No corpus file, dump, PDF used as data, audio, model artifact, or dataset archive was downloaded.
- Source-family records are discovery containers and cannot be selected for collection.
- A public endpoint, license label, or research paper is not sufficient by itself for collection, derived use, redistribution, or scientific inclusion.
- `CONDITIONAL` means “worth further review,” not “approved.”
