# Code of Conduct

KreyolBench is a public-interest research project. We expect collaborators to treat each other, Haitian Creole speakers, annotators, community contributors, and partner institutions with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Credit contributors fairly.
- Welcome people from different countries, disciplines, institutions, and experience levels.
- Be careful with community language data and cultural context.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Document decisions and assumptions.
- Report risks early.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on nationality, race, ethnicity, language, gender, religion, disability, migration status, institution, or career stage.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Publishing or sharing private data without permission.
- Pressuring contributors for unpaid labor beyond agreed scope.
- Using project data or tools for surveillance, exclusion, or harmful profiling.
- Making public claims on behalf of KreyolBench without authorization.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project lead or designated maintainers. If the concern involves a maintainer, report directly to the project lead or an agreed external advisor.

Reports should include:

- What happened.
- Who was involved.
- When it happened.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or documents if available.
- Whether immediate action is needed.

## Enforcement

Possible actions include:

- Private warning.
- Removal from a discussion or working group.
- Revocation of repository or data access.
- Removal from authorship consideration when misconduct invalidates contribution.
- Public clarification if project reputation or community trust is affected.
- Escalation to institutional or legal channels when appropriate.
